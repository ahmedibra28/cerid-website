CeRID places great importance on promoting lifelong learning, knowledge transfer, and capacity building through various educational initiatives. The organization focuses on the following key aspects:

1. **Supportive Centers and Schools**: CeRID establishes and manages supportive centers and schools that serve as hubs for knowledge transfer and capacity building. These centers and schools are designed to provide a conducive learning environment and offer relevant educational programs to individuals of all ages.

2. **Vocational Education**: CeRID recognizes the importance of vocational education in empowering youth and equipping them with the necessary skills for employment and career advancement. The organization ensures that youth have access to quality vocational education programs that align with market demands. By providing vocational training opportunities, CeRID aims to bridge the skills gap and enhance employability.

3. **Industrial Park Engagement**: CeRID actively engages youth in the industrial park, providing them with practical work experience and opportunities to apply their knowledge and abilities. By working in the industrial park, youth can further develop their skills, gain industry-specific knowledge, and contribute to economic growth.

CeRID's commitment to education and skill development extends beyond formal schooling. The organization embraces a holistic approach that emphasizes lifelong learning, practical experiences, and engagement with various sectors. Through these efforts, CeRID aims to empower individuals, enhance their knowledge and abilities, and ensure they are equipped to thrive in the ever-evolving labor market.
