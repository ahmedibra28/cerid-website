CeRID is actively involved in research and studies pertaining to the productive sector, resilience, and adaptation to climate change. The organization conducts research to gather valuable insights and knowledge that can inform decision-making and guide appropriate responses. CeRID's research efforts focus on several key areas, including:

1. **Appropriate Agroecological Adaptable Seeds Value Chain**: CeRID engages in research related to developing an appropriate value chain for agroecological adaptable seeds. This research aims to identify and promote seeds that are well-suited to local environmental conditions, enhance crop productivity, and improve resilience to climate change impacts. By focusing on seed varieties that are adaptable to changing climatic conditions, CeRID seeks to support sustainable agriculture and food security.

2. **Strengthening Pastoralist Livelihoods in the Greater Horn of Africa**: CeRID conducts research on strategies for strengthening pastoralist livelihoods in the Greater Horn of Africa. This research explores innovative approaches and effective anticipatory actions to address the challenges faced by pastoralist communities in the region. By understanding the unique needs and dynamics of pastoralist livelihoods, CeRID aims to develop targeted interventions that enhance their resilience and well-being.

CeRID collaborates with various partners, including the Intergovernmental Authority on Development (IGAD), to carry out successful research initiatives. By working in partnership, CeRID leverages expertise and resources to achieve comprehensive and impactful outcomes. Through its research endeavors, CeRID aims to contribute to evidence-based decision-making, policy formulation, and the implementation of sustainable solutions that address the complex challenges of the region.
