CeRID is committed to alleviating human suffering by promoting equitable economic and social development, human rights, productivity, and income through knowledge and skill enhancement. The organization recognizes the importance of empowering women in this process. CeRID focuses on the following initiatives:

1. **Micro-loans for Women**: CeRID provides access to micro-loans for women, enabling them to build their enterprises and contribute to economic growth. These micro-loans offer financial support and opportunities for women to start or expand their businesses, fostering entrepreneurship and self-reliance.

2. **Basic Education**: CeRID recognizes the significance of education in empowering individuals and communities. In line with this, the organization promotes basic education for women, ensuring they have access to fundamental knowledge and skills. By enhancing women's education, CeRID aims to improve their economic prospects and overall well-being.

CeRID's efforts aim to create an enabling environment where women can actively participate in economic activities, exercise their rights, and achieve financial independence. By addressing the barriers and challenges faced by women, CeRID strives to foster equitable development, empower individuals, and contribute to the overall prosperity of communities.
