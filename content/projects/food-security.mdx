The Food Security project implemented by the Centre for Research and Integrated Development (CeRID) in partnership with Trócaire and supported by Development and Peace-Caritas Canada focuses on improving food security for vulnerable IDP (Internally Displaced Persons) and host communities in Luuq district, Gedo. The project aims to sustain the gains achieved in the pilot phase of a food production project supported by Trócaire.

## Target Beneficiaries and Activities

The project primarily targets 150 vulnerable women and their households from IDP and host communities in Luuq district. Additionally, 90 students studying agriculture at the Luuq Agricultural Training Centre (ATC) are also included as beneficiaries.

The project has the following expected results:

1. **Improved Nutrition Outcomes**: The project aims to improve nutrition outcomes for children under the age of 5 and pregnant and lactating women from IDP and host communities. This includes interventions to ensure access to nutritious food and address malnutrition.

2. **Improved Food Security**: The project aims to enhance food security for the target households. This involves a range of activities aimed at improving food production and access to nutritious food.

3. **Knowledge Enhancement**: The project focuses on improving knowledge related to food production. Both the targeted women beneficiaries and students from the Luuq ATC receive training on environmentally sound food production techniques. The students also gain hands-on experience through an attachment program with farming groups.

## Activities and Support

The project engages the target beneficiaries in various activities to improve food production and access to nutritious food. These activities include:

1. **Provision of Agricultural Inputs**: The project provides agricultural inputs, including diverse crop seeds, to support the targeted women and their households in their food production efforts.

2. **Trainings**: Training sessions are conducted to educate the beneficiaries on environmentally sustainable food production techniques. This includes promoting practices that conserve natural resources and enhance agricultural productivity.

3. **Cash for Work Initiative**: The project implements a cash-for-work initiative, which provides income-generating opportunities to the beneficiaries. This initiative helps address immediate economic needs while also contributing to food security.

4. **Livelihood Support**: The project offers livelihood support to the beneficiaries, which may include assistance in income-generating activities or the provision of resources to improve their overall livelihoods.

5. **Community-Based Savings and Loan Groups**: The project facilitates the formation of community-based savings and loan groups, providing informal financial services to the beneficiaries. This helps strengthen their financial resilience and supports their food security efforts.

Through these activities and support mechanisms, the project aims to strengthen the resilience of the targeted communities, enabling them to cope with recurring humanitarian shocks and stresses related to food security.
