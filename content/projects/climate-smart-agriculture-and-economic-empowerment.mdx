## Project Overview

CeRID implemented the Climate Smart Agricultural Value Chain and Economic Empowerment programme in partnership with NCA–NORAD. The three-year initiative responded to youth unemployment, climate change, and food insecurity through vocational education, agricultural support, and community resilience planning.

## Overall Goal

To equip marginalised people and communities with the skills, resources, and support needed to build sustainable livelihoods and resilience to a changing climate.

## Key Objectives

1. Prepare young people for employment and financial independence through vocational skills training.
2. Strengthen small-scale farmers with training, certified seeds, farm inputs, and access to land.
3. Connect participants with financial institutions and private-sector opportunities.
4. Establish community-managed disaster risk reduction plans and climate early-warning systems.
5. Strengthen government participation in agricultural extension and supportive policy.

## Emerging Impact

By combining vocational training with climate-smart agricultural support, the programme created two complementary pathways to stronger livelihoods. Youth gained job-ready skills while farmers improved access to knowledge and productive resources, and communities strengthened their planning for climate-related risks.
