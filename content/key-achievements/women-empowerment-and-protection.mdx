At CERID, we understand that women and girls are the cornerstones of strong communities. That's why, since 2019, we've been dedicated to empowering them through a comprehensive program that addresses social, economic, and legal challenges.

**Reaching 6,000 Women and Girls:**

Our program has already provided vital support to over 6,000 adolescent girls and women in Somalia. We offer a holistic approach that includes:

* **Safe Spaces:** Providing safe havens where girls and women can access social support, build networks, and participate in recreational activities.
* **Life Skills Training:** Equipping participants with valuable skills like financial literacy, entrepreneurship, negotiation, and decision-making.
* **Mental Health Support:** Addressing the emotional well-being of girls and women who may have faced trauma or hardship. 
* **Cash and Voucher Assistance for GBV Survivors:**  Providing financial resources to survivors of gender-based violence to meet essential needs and support their recovery. 
* **Legal Support:** Providing legal aid and education on women's rights to help them navigate the legal system and access justice.

**Building a Supportive Network:**

CERID recognizes the importance of community-wide engagement. Our outreach programs target:

* **Religious and Traditional Leaders:** Fostering understanding and collaboration to address issues like child marriage and gender-based violence.
* **Boys and Men:** Encouraging positive attitudes towards gender equality and promoting their role in supporting women and girls.

**Preventing Child Marriage:**

CERID's Child Early and Forced Marriage (CEFM) protection initiative specifically targets adolescent girls in schools. Through awareness campaigns, mentorship programs, and alternative education opportunities, we strive to:

* **Reduce Early Marriage Rates:** Equipping girls with knowledge and skills to advocate for themselves and delay marriage until they are ready.
* **Keep Girls in School:** Our program managers have successfully kept over 2,000 girls at risk of dropping out due to early marriage pressure, in school.
