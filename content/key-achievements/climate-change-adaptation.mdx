**Empowering for the Future:** CERID's climate change mitigation program has successfully transformed the practices of over 2,000 Somali farmers and pastoralists. This achievement equips these vital communities with the knowledge and tools they need to adapt and build resilience in the face of climate challenges.

**Building Climate-Smart Solutions:** The program focuses on climate-smart agriculture (CSA) and agropastoralism, providing targeted training in sustainable farming techniques, water management, and soil health improvement. Additionally, it equips communities with a clear understanding of climate change and its local impacts.

**Investing in Women and Youth:** Recognizing the disproportionate impact climate change has on vulnerable groups, CERID prioritizes empowering women and youth. The program offers specific training and resources to ensure they have the skills and resources to thrive in a climate-resilient future.

**A Flourishing Future:** CERID's commitment is to a future where Somali farmers and pastoralists can face climate change with confidence. These achievements demonstrate the program's success in building a more resilient Somalia, where communities not only survive but flourish.
Expanding Our Impact: With your help, CERID can empower even more farmers and pastoralists to adopt climate-smart practices. This will strengthen communities across Somalia, equipping them to not only survive but thrive in a changing climate.
